# Director Grammar Reference

Use these profiles as starting points, not rigid claims about every film. Focal lengths are approximate full-frame equivalents. Translate names into traits before sending the image prompt.

## Hirokazu Kore-eda

- Strong signature lock: 50mm eye-level domestic observation + doorway-layered family blocking + soft window daylight + restrained natural color + emotion carried by an unfinished household gesture.
- Dramatic mode: ordinary family life, withheld emotion, gestures and domestic routines carrying conflict.
- Frame: observational medium wides, doorways and adjacent rooms, gentle asymmetry, people sharing lived-in space.
- Camera: eye level or slightly low, patient and unobtrusive, mostly still with modest motivated movement.
- Lens: default 40mm or 50mm; 65–85mm for private observation without glamour.
- Focus: moderate depth so environment and relationships remain legible.
- Light: soft window daylight, overcast exteriors, practical household warmth, restrained contrast.
- Palette/texture: natural skin, wood, laundry whites, muted greens and browns, subtle film grain.
- Avoid: melodramatic posing, aggressive color grades, heroic close-ups, conspicuous camera tricks.

## Wong Kar-wai

- Strong signature lock: 28mm close-wide proximity + glass/reflection/foreground obstruction + cyan-green practical light against restrained amber + pushed grain, halation, and selective motion smear + lovers displaced within the same frame.
- Dramatic mode: longing, missed timing, urban solitude, bodies near each other but emotionally displaced.
- Frame: close wide-angle proximity, reflections, glass, doorframes, foreground occlusion, compressed city layers.
- Camera: intimate handheld drift or lateral motion; selective step-printed motion impression when action supports it.
- Lens: default 28mm or 35mm close to subjects; 50–85mm for compressed private glances.
- Focus: shallow-to-moderate with layered practical lights, but keep the emotional gesture readable.
- Light: mixed neon/practical tungsten/fluorescent sources, underexposed edges, humid night atmosphere.
- Palette/texture: saturated red, green, amber, or cyan with one dominant family; pushed grain, halation, motion smear.
- Avoid: neon cyberpunk spectacle, random Dutch angles, glossy music-video polish, copying a known corridor or costume.

## Yasujirō Ozu

- Strong signature lock: 50mm low seated locked camera + threshold-based domestic geometry + moderate-deep focus + even soft interior light + one small structural red accent and withheld reaction.
- Dramatic mode: generational change, domestic ritual, emotional restraint, time visible in rooms.
- Frame: low seated camera, frontal or lateral room geometry, precise thresholds, balanced negative space.
- Camera: locked-off; cuts replace camera movement.
- Lens: default 40mm or 50mm with restrained perspective.
- Focus: moderate-to-deep; architecture and objects remain meaningful.
- Light: even soft interior daylight, controlled tonal separation, minimal spectacle.
- Palette/texture: quiet domestic colors, small red accent, clean but tactile film response.
- Avoid: floating camera, shallow-focus glamour, exaggerated wides, sentimental visual effects.

## Edward Yang

- Strong signature lock: 35mm locked deep tableau + figures embedded in modern architecture + windows/reflections across three planes + neutral urban practical light + simultaneous actions exposing social distance.
- Dramatic mode: individuals embedded in modern systems, family and city life sharing the frame.
- Frame: deep layered tableaux, windows and reflections, multiple planes, characters small within architecture.
- Camera: patient locked-off observation; rare movement is spatially motivated.
- Lens: default 35mm or 50mm; 28mm for complex architectural staging.
- Focus: deep or moderate-deep so simultaneous actions remain readable.
- Light: natural urban daylight, practical office/apartment night sources, restrained contrast.
- Palette/texture: neutral concrete, glass, pale fluorescents, quiet pockets of color.
- Avoid: coverage-style close-ups, forced symbolism, shallow bokeh, melodramatic grading.

## Hou Hsiao-hsien

- Strong signature lock: 35mm distant locked tableau + action partially hidden by thresholds or fabric + moderate-deep atmospheric focus + motivated low-key practical light + memory expressed through duration and people leaving frame.
- Dramatic mode: memory, history, duration, people partially obscured by lived space.
- Frame: distant tableaux, layered interiors, thresholds, curtains, smoke or foliage as soft partitions.
- Camera: locked-off or extremely slow movement; allow action to enter and leave the frame.
- Lens: default 35mm or 50mm; 28mm for spatially dense interiors.
- Focus: moderate-to-deep with gentle atmospheric separation.
- Light: motivated daylight and practicals, low-key interiors, natural falloff.
- Palette/texture: earthy muted colors, restrained red/gold accents, fine grain and soft highlight roll-off.
- Avoid: emphatic reaction shots, showy tracking, exposition-heavy props, artificial rim lighting.

## Park Chan-wook

- Strong signature lock: 28mm designed architectural geometry + exact controlled camera implication + sculpted motivated color separation + tactile dark red/green materials + one precise object or spatial reversal carrying tension.
- Dramatic mode: controlled tension, desire, moral reversal, graphic cause-and-effect.
- Frame: precise geometry, architectural frames, unusual but purposeful vantage points, elegant object inserts.
- Camera: exact dolly, crane, or lateral motion implication; stillness before visual release.
- Lens: default 28mm or 35mm for designed space; 75–100mm for compressed inserts and surveillance.
- Focus: deliberate plane selection; either deep graphic staging or exact isolated detail.
- Light: sculpted motivated sources, strong color separation, polished shadow control.
- Palette/texture: lacquered darks, wine red, forest green, cream, tactile fabric and wood.
- Avoid: gore as decoration, random symmetry, generic luxury advertising, empty visual cleverness.

## Stanley Kubrick

- Strong signature lock: 24mm one-point perspective + centered human figure dwarfed by architecture + deep focus + inexorable locked or axial camera logic + institutional light and ritualized blocking.
- Dramatic mode: systems overpowering individuals, ritual, dread, absurd control.
- Frame: one-point perspective, centered corridors, architectural scale, formal negative space.
- Camera: locked symmetry or slow inexorable dolly.
- Lens: default 24mm or 28mm; 18–21mm for controlled spatial distortion.
- Focus: deep enough to expose the whole system around the subject.
- Light: practical-source realism or hard institutional clarity, carefully controlled exposure.
- Palette/texture: disciplined limited palette, clean geometry, occasional saturated structural color.
- Avoid: copying iconic hotel corridors, spacesuits, costumes, or recognizable production design.

## David Fincher

- Strong signature lock: 35mm mechanically exact framing + controlled negative space and foreground layers + low-key motivated practicals with readable shadows + cool green-gray restraint + movement synchronized to information.
- Dramatic mode: procedural control, paranoia, hidden systems, emotional coldness.
- Frame: exact headroom, controlled negative space, foreground layers, level horizons, dark readable environments.
- Camera: mechanically smooth dolly/pan or immovable precision; movement follows information.
- Lens: default 35mm or 40mm; 27–32mm for environmental control; 65–85mm for surveillance compression.
- Focus: moderate, meticulously placed; no arbitrary focus hunting.
- Light: low-key motivated practicals, soft top/side light, protected highlights, deep detailed shadows.
- Palette/texture: cool green-gray or sodium warmth, restrained saturation, clean digital texture with subtle grain.
- Avoid: crushed unreadable blacks, handheld chaos, flashy lens flares, generic thriller teal-orange.

## Wes Anderson

- Strong signature lock: 32mm frontal symmetry + flat lateral blocking + moderate-deep focus + even designed light + strict limited palette and deadpan object choreography, without copying signature IP.
- Dramatic mode: emotional distance expressed through theatrical order, collections, rituals, and deadpan behavior.
- Frame: frontal symmetry, lateral profile staging, flat architectural layers, centered objects.
- Camera: locked frontal view, precise lateral tracking, or axial move.
- Lens: default 27mm, 32mm, or 40mm depending subject scale.
- Focus: moderate-to-deep, keeping the designed world legible.
- Light: even storybook daylight or clean motivated interiors.
- Palette/texture: controlled limited palette, tactile miniatures/sets, period materials, restrained graphic color blocks.
- Avoid: copying a known hotel, uniform, typography system, or character; avoid turning every frame into cute whimsy.

## Christopher Nolan

- Strong signature lock: 35mm grounded large-scale staging + human foreground against practical architecture + moderate-deep spatial clarity + hard natural or practical light + physical urgency without fantasy glow.
- Dramatic mode: urgent physical stakes, time pressure, individuals facing systems larger than themselves.
- Frame: large-scale spatial clarity, strong horizon, practical environments, foreground human scale against architecture.
- Camera: grounded handheld for immediacy or precise large-format movement; physical rather than weightless.
- Lens: default 35mm or 50mm; 24–28mm for environmental scale; 65–85mm for compressed urgency.
- Focus: moderate-to-deep, crisp spatial information.
- Light: naturalistic hard daylight, practical night sources, high dynamic range without fantasy glow.
- Palette/texture: neutral/cool realism, dense blacks, photochemical large-format clarity and grain.
- Avoid: copying recognizable masks, vehicles, dream-city imagery, or franchise production design.

## Denis Villeneuve

- Strong signature lock: 28mm monumental negative space + small isolated human silhouette + deep atmospheric separation + directional natural light through haze + mineral palette and ritual stillness.
- Dramatic mode: awe, isolation, ritual, human figures dwarfed by environment.
- Frame: monumental negative space, severe geometry, silhouettes, slow visual hierarchy.
- Camera: still or slow controlled movement, often at human eye level against massive scale.
- Lens: default 28mm or 35mm for scale; 50–75mm for compressed introspection.
- Focus: deep-to-moderate with atmospheric separation rather than artificial bokeh.
- Light: directional natural light, haze, overcast diffusion, strong silhouette logic.
- Palette/texture: mineral neutrals, sand, charcoal, fog blue, one muted material accent, clean grain.
- Avoid: copying desert costumes, spacecraft, orange dust worlds, or recognizable franchise architecture.

## Sofia Coppola

- Strong signature lock: 50mm intimate off-center observation + mirror/window separation + shallow-to-moderate environmental focus + available dusk or practical light + soft bloom and private emotional drift.
- Dramatic mode: private loneliness inside privilege, drifting youth, quiet alienation.
- Frame: intimate observation, mirrors, hotel rooms, beds/windows, soft off-center negative space.
- Camera: gentle handheld or still observation, close but non-invasive.
- Lens: default 40mm or 50mm; 75–85mm for private distance.
- Focus: shallow-to-moderate, retaining environmental mood.
- Light: available window light, dusk, practical lamps, soft overexposure or pastel night ambience.
- Palette/texture: powdery neutrals, faded pink/blue, natural skin, gentle grain and highlight bloom.
- Avoid: fashion-campaign posing, sugary decoration, glamour retouching, copying known hotel imagery.

## Akira Kurosawa

- Strong signature lock: 28mm multi-plane action + bold diagonal blocking + deep focus + directional weather and hard tonal separation + decisive movement crossing the frame.
- Dramatic mode: moral pressure, group dynamics, duty, conflict made visible through weather and physical direction.
- Frame: triangular groups, strong horizons, telephoto weather layers, foreground bodies against active landscape.
- Camera: locked clarity, lateral tracking, or forceful axial movement; action has readable direction.
- Lens: 24–35mm for spatial action; 100–135mm for compressed rain, dust, crowds, or faces.
- Focus: deep staging or compressed layered action, never decorative bokeh.
- Light: hard sun, high-contrast overcast, rain, snow, wind, smoke, and backlit atmosphere.
- Palette/texture: disciplined earth, black, gray, white, weathered cloth, one heraldic accent when motivated.
- Avoid: copying samurai costumes, battle staging, iconic poses, or using weather without dramatic function.

## Andrei Tarkovsky

- Strong signature lock: 28mm patient layered space + reflective water or threshold foreground + slow visual drift + natural low-contrast light + time expressed through material decay and suspended gesture.
- Dramatic mode: memory, faith, sacrifice, dream and reality sharing physical space.
- Frame: long-depth interiors, doorways, water, smoke, wind in vegetation, figures crossing slowly through layers.
- Camera: still or nearly imperceptible drift; movement reveals duration rather than coverage.
- Lens: 24–35mm for immersive space; 50–85mm for contemplative separation.
- Focus: deep-to-moderate, with atmosphere and reflections carrying transitions.
- Light: overcast daylight, dim windows, fire or practicals, soft falloff and moist surfaces.
- Palette/texture: mineral gray, moss, oxidized metal, wet earth, muted gold, occasional memory-shift color system.
- Avoid: copying famous rooms, levitation, mirror arrangements, or symbolic props without narrative cause.

## Ingmar Bergman

- Strong signature lock: 85mm face geometry + exact eye-line opposition + sparse dark background + sculpted side light + emotion held in micro-expression and silence.
- Dramatic mode: identity, intimacy, guilt, faith, psychological confrontation.
- Frame: severe close portraits, paired profiles, faces separated by darkness, sparse rooms and precise negative space.
- Camera: still, direct, and unprotective; movement is rare and psychologically motivated.
- Lens: 50–58mm for human proximity; 85–100mm for compressed facial confrontation.
- Focus: exact eyes and skin plane, controlled falloff, no glamour softness.
- Light: hard window edge, soft frontal exposure, or deep black separation with protected skin texture.
- Palette/texture: monochrome or restrained flesh, charcoal, bone, muted blue-gray, tactile grain.
- Avoid: beauty retouching, theatrical crying, generic noir, or copying recognizable dual-face compositions.

## Federico Fellini

- Strong signature lock: 24mm theatrical wide proximity + carnival-like lateral procession + expressive faces across depth + hard studio-practical mix + reality tipping into social fantasy.
- Dramatic mode: spectacle, memory, desire, public performance, grotesque tenderness.
- Frame: crowded tableaux, curved blocking, frontal entrances, oversized props, faces distributed across theatrical layers.
- Camera: floating track, crane implication, or energized locked proscenium.
- Lens: 21–28mm for expressive proximity; 35mm for ensemble rhythm; 75mm for isolated witness.
- Focus: moderate-deep so social spectacle remains legible.
- Light: designed studio moonlight, hard practical pools, luminous haze, theatrical contrast.
- Palette/texture: black, cream, circus red, powder blue, smoky silver, textured costumes and painted surfaces.
- Avoid: random surreal clutter, direct circus imagery, copied costumes, or grotesque bodies without humanity.

## Alfred Hitchcock

- Strong signature lock: 35mm subjective geometry + one precisely planted visual clue + controlled frame-within-frame + motivated suspense light + gaze and camera position revealing dangerous knowledge.
- Dramatic mode: suspense, mistaken identity, voyeurism, ordinary spaces becoming traps.
- Frame: stairways, windows, doors, overhead patterns, controlled negative space, object inserts with causal meaning.
- Camera: subjective push, exact pan, overhead reveal, or locked point-of-view relation.
- Lens: 24–35mm for spatial suspense; 50mm for objective staging; 85–100mm for clue or surveillance compression.
- Focus: deep enough to plant the threat, or exact rack endpoint from observer to clue.
- Light: motivated practical contrast, silhouette, shadow pattern, restrained color separation.
- Palette/texture: controlled primaries, cream, charcoal, cool green or blue, one warning accent.
- Avoid: copying famous staircases, shower imagery, birds, silhouettes, costumes, or twist-specific props.

## Sergio Leone

- Strong signature lock: extreme 24mm landscape against 180mm facial detail + long stillness before decisive motion + low horizon + hard sun and dust + tactile eyes, hands, leather, and metal.
- Dramatic mode: waiting, ritualized confrontation, greed, mythic scale, time stretched before action.
- Frame: extreme wides, extreme close details, strong horizon, triangular confrontation, empty lateral distance.
- Camera: monumental stillness, slow push, or precise lateral reveal.
- Lens: 24–28mm for landscape scale; 50mm for body ritual; 135–180mm for eyes, hands, and compressed confrontation.
- Focus: deep landscape or razor-specific telephoto detail; avoid generic portrait blur.
- Light: hard directional sun, dusty backlight, dense shade, tungsten night practicals.
- Palette/texture: sun-baked ochre, tobacco brown, faded blue, iron black, sweat, dust, worn leather and wood.
- Avoid: copying western costumes, gun belts, iconic standoffs, or using extreme close-ups without causal tension.

## Agnès Varda

- Strong signature lock: 28mm curious close observation + playful object-person relation + documentary daylight + graphic found color + social detail turning into visual essay.
- Dramatic mode: everyday life, memory, feminism, community, chance, affectionate social observation.
- Frame: direct portraits in environment, lateral street discovery, mirrors, found signs without readable branding, object rhymes.
- Camera: handheld curiosity, still portrait, or modest lateral movement close to lived space.
- Lens: 24–35mm for participatory proximity; 50mm for respectful portrait; 85mm for found detail.
- Focus: flexible deep-to-selective focus, always preserving the social context.
- Light: available daylight, simple bounce, honest weather, occasional graphic artificial color.
- Palette/texture: sea blue, sun yellow, red accent, concrete, skin, patterned fabric, tactile documentary grain.
- Avoid: whimsical decoration without social meaning, copied beach imagery, or polished advertising realism.

## Chantal Akerman

- Strong signature lock: 35mm frontal locked duration + rectilinear domestic or urban compartment + deep focus + flat available light + action repeating within an unyielding frame.
- Dramatic mode: routine, waiting, labor, displacement, duration and off-screen pressure.
- Frame: frontal rooms, corridors, tables, streets, strong horizontal and vertical partitions, deliberate empty time.
- Camera: locked-off, fixed height, minimal reframing; cuts preserve spatial rigor.
- Lens: 28–35mm for rooms and streets; 50mm for restrained human scale; 85mm only for distant compression.
- Focus: deep or moderate-deep; space remains accountable.
- Light: available windows, fluorescent interiors, neutral practicals, low spectacle.
- Palette/texture: beige, muted green, brown, institutional blue, off-white, restrained red objects.
- Avoid: beautifying routine, expressive coverage, decorative symmetry, or dramatic grading.

## Bong Joon-ho

- Strong signature lock: 28mm class-coded architecture + multi-level blocking + exact tonal shift within one frame + motivated genre light + foreground clue and background consequence both readable.
- Dramatic mode: class systems, family survival, social satire turning into physical danger.
- Frame: stairs, vertical levels, thresholds, ensemble triangles, foreground jokes with background threat.
- Camera: precise dolly or pan following information; grounded handheld only when order breaks.
- Lens: 24–35mm for spatial cause-and-effect; 50mm for ensemble relation; 85–100mm for surveillance or clue compression.
- Focus: deep relational staging or deliberate rack between class-coded planes.
- Light: naturalistic architecture, fluorescent institutions, hard rain, controlled night practicals.
- Palette/texture: concrete gray, damp green, warm domestic amber, black, one class-coded saturated accent.
- Avoid: copying basements, specific houses, creatures, uniforms, or turning social contrast into generic thriller style.

## Pedro Almodóvar

- Strong signature lock: 50mm graphic emotional staging + saturated primary color blocks + precise domestic production design + clean frontal or profile light + melodrama held in composed bodies and objects.
- Dramatic mode: desire, identity, family secrets, performance, pain expressed through vivid domestic worlds.
- Frame: graphic interiors, mirrors, doorways, frontal portraits, object inserts, patterned surfaces with controlled hierarchy.
- Camera: exact stillness, clean dolly, or purposeful lateral reveal.
- Lens: 28–35mm for designed rooms; 50mm for human drama; 85–100mm for portrait or object isolation.
- Focus: clean selective planes with readable color fields.
- Light: polished motivated interiors, soft frontal skin, saturated practical or daylight separation.
- Palette/texture: vermilion, cobalt, mustard, emerald, black, cream, glossy tile, fabric, lacquer, printed pattern.
- Avoid: random rainbow interiors, fashion-ad posing, copied apartments, or color without emotional function.

## Terrence Malick

- Strong signature lock: 21mm close-moving natural perspective + low-angle bodies against sky or grass + deep available-light space + sun flare from a visible source + gesture, wind, and environment carrying interior thought.
- Dramatic mode: memory, grace, nature, war, love, transient human presence within landscape.
- Frame: low horizons, hands brushing plants, backlit figures, wide environmental proximity, drifting relational fragments.
- Camera: fluid handheld or stabilized wandering close to bodies; movement follows touch, wind, and light.
- Lens: 18–24mm for immersive proximity; 28mm for human landscape; 50–85mm only for quiet compression.
- Focus: deep natural space with occasional close-focus transition; avoid artificial bokeh.
- Light: dawn, dusk, backlight, overcast softness, motivated sun flare, natural exposure shifts.
- Palette/texture: living green, wheat gold, sky cyan, earth, natural skin, luminous highlights and organic grain.
- Avoid: empty nature montage, copied field imagery, constant flare, whispering symbolism, or weightless gimbal polish.

## Zhang Yimou

- Strong signature lock: 28mm ceremonial mass geometry + one dominant saturated color field + deep graphic staging + directional hard light or weather + individual body positioned against collective order.
- Dramatic mode: ritual, sacrifice, desire, power, individuals constrained by spectacle and social order.
- Frame: repeated bodies, banners, courtyards, strong diagonals, centered ceremony, landscape as graphic field.
- Camera: locked monumental view, exact crane implication, lateral procession, or controlled axial move.
- Lens: 24–35mm for mass geometry; 50mm for ritual intimacy; 85–135mm for compressed repetition and landscape layers.
- Focus: deep ceremonial clarity or compressed graphic planes.
- Light: hard sun, fog, snow, fire, colored fabric bounce, strong silhouette separation.
- Palette/texture: select one dominant system—vermilion/black, indigo/earth, gold/charcoal, or snow white/red accent.
- Avoid: mixing several color systems, copying palace or martial imagery, ornamental crowds without dramatic hierarchy.

## Blending Rules

- Use one primary grammar for composition, lens, and camera.
- Use at most one secondary grammar for light, palette, or texture.
- Never average focal lengths. Select one lens from the primary grammar.
- Resolve contradictions explicitly. Example: “Kore-eda blocking and 50mm observation; Wong-derived mixed practical color only.”
- Keep the scene original and do not import signature sets, costumes, actors, or props from films.
