# Reference Frame Gallery

These 24 original 2.39:1 frames (images not bundled with cinema-dna; written benchmarks only) use two shared narrative tests to make differences in composition, lens, light, palette, and emotional behavior easy to compare:

- **Original set:** one person waiting alone at a railway station.
- **Later-added set:** at dawn after a town festival, a lone street cleaner pauses in an empty square as wind lifts rows of white paper lanterns and leaves one red lantern at their feet.

Use the written benchmarks as quality targets, not templates to reproduce. Read the corresponding written profile in `director-grammars.md` and `director-color-signatures.md` before prompting.
