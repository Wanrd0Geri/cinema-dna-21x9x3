# Style Amplification

Use this reference at 90/100 or 100/100 director-language strength. Stronger translation means clearer formal decisions, not copying a known film or applying more effects.

## Seven-Axis Score

Score each axis as absent, supporting, or dominant:

1. composition and blocking;
2. camera position, focal length, and movement;
3. motivated lighting;
4. palette and capture texture;
5. production design, material, and wardrobe;
6. temporal behavior, shutter, and movement rhythm;
7. emotional distance and performance behavior.

At 90/100, make all seven visible and exactly four dominant. At 100/100, keep all seven visible, choose four or five dominant, and exaggerate exactly one lever.

## Controlled Exaggeration Levers

- **Lens proximity:** move the camera unusually close with a compatible wide lens, or unusually far with a compatible long lens. Preserve facial plausibility.
- **Negative-space scale:** enlarge active emptiness, architecture, weather, or darkness while retaining a clear narrative anchor.
- **Color-field dominance:** let one physical environmental hue occupy 55–70% of the frame; keep skin and motivated sources plausible.
- **Camera rigidity:** make symmetry, low height, locked framing, axial motion, or mechanical precision unusually strict.
- **Temporal behavior:** use selective motion smear, suspended stillness, action entering/leaving frame, or a frozen micro-gesture.
- **Production repetition:** repeat one shape, prop class, color block, doorway, lamp, textile, or architectural module to create authored order.

Never exaggerate more than one lever. Keep other axes coherent and subordinate.

## Restraint Is a Signature

- For observational or minimalist grammars, amplify by removing coverage, decorative light, saturated accents, and expressive camera moves.
- For graphic grammars, amplify structure, color distribution, and object choreography before adding spectacle.
- For atmospheric grammars, amplify depth, weather, falloff, duration, and silhouette before adding props.
- For kinetic grammars, amplify directional blocking, foreground pressure, shutter behavior, and movement path before adding camera shake.

## Failure Gate

Regenerate once when the frame:

- depends on grain, bloom, flare, or a global tint to signal style;
- combines several famous motifs into imitation;
- makes every axis equally loud;
- loses the narrative instant beneath decoration;
- could swap to another director by changing only the grade;
- uses maximal saturation where the grammar depends on restraint.
