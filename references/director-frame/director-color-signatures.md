# Director Color Signatures

Use these palettes as controllable starting recipes, not rigid claims about every film. Adapt the recipe to time, weather, location, and story while preserving its hue relationships, color distribution, and exposure logic. Translate names into color instructions before generation.

## Color Recipe Rules

Define five elements:

1. **Dominant field:** 40–65% of the frame through architecture, weather, large surfaces, or ambient light.
2. **Supporting family:** 15–30% through wardrobe, practical light, furniture, landscape, or secondary surfaces.
3. **Accent:** 3–10% near the primary or secondary anchor; keep it deliberate.
4. **Neutral base:** blacks, whites, grays, skin, wood, concrete, or shadow that prevents color noise.
5. **Exposure behavior:** bright/flat, low-key, underexposed edges, protected highlights, lifted blacks, or dense photochemical contrast.

Use approximate shares, not literal segmentation. Color should arise from physical carriers and motivated sources. Keep skin plausible.

## Hirokazu Kore-eda

- Signature: cool seasonal daylight balanced by quiet household warmth; natural materials; one modest memory-bearing accent.
- Default recipe: rainy blue-gray or soft leaf green dominant; warm wood and natural skin support; off-white neutral; mustard yellow, faded coral, or small red accent.
- Distribution: 50% weather/daylight and walls, 25% wood/skin, 20% off-white/gray, 5% accent object.
- Exposure: soft low-to-medium contrast, open shadows, restrained highlights, no heavy grade.
- Carriers: windows, laundry, wood furniture, worn cotton, food, school objects, umbrellas, plants.
- Avoid: saturated full-frame color wash, glossy warm nostalgia, aggressive complementary contrast.

## Wong Kar-wai

- Signature: saturated cyan-green or emerald night field against dirty amber skin/practicals, with deep red or magenta punctuation and dense black separation.
- Default recipe: 40–50% cyan-green/emerald, 20–25% amber/tungsten, 20–30% humid black, 5–8% deep red or magenta.
- Exposure: underexposed edges, luminous practicals, halation, pushed grain, selective color smear.
- Carriers: fluorescent tubes, glass, tiled walls, reflections, wet streets, wardrobe edges, signs without readable branding.
- Avoid: evenly colorful cyberpunk, rainbow neon, clean teal-orange blockbuster grading.

## Yasujirō Ozu

- Signature: warm cream, natural wood, muted green or blue panels, and one clear vermilion structural accent.
- Default recipe: 40–50% cream/wood, 25–30% muted green or blue, 15–20% gray/black, 5–8% vermilion red.
- Exposure: even soft light, clean midtones, gentle film density, minimal color cast.
- Carriers: walls, sliding doors, ceramics, cushions, kettles, signs, flowers, clothing details.
- Avoid: pastel fantasy, dramatic complementary lighting, large red wash instead of precise red structure.

## Edward Yang

- Signature: cool modern neutrals, green-cyan glass and fluorescents, pale concrete, with small pockets of warm domestic or primary color.
- Default recipe: 45–55% concrete gray/off-white, 20–30% cool green-cyan glass, 10–20% warm practical amber, 3–7% red/blue/yellow object accent.
- Exposure: restrained contrast, clear deep space, practical night highlights, neutral skin protected from green contamination.
- Carriers: windows, offices, apartment walls, street reflections, monitors, clothes, traffic or household objects.
- Avoid: monochrome blue wash, shallow neon spectacle, overly warm sentimental grading.

## Hou Hsiao-hsien

- Signature: earthy olive, umber, smoke gray, aged gold, and sparing crimson embedded in atmospheric depth.
- Default recipe: 35–45% olive/umber, 20–30% smoke gray, 15–25% aged gold/warm practicals, 3–6% deep crimson.
- Exposure: low-key natural falloff, soft highlight roll-off, atmospheric separation, dense but readable shadows.
- Carriers: timber, curtains, foliage, incense/smoke, old walls, lamps, fabric, table objects.
- Avoid: clean digital color blocks, bright red dominance, high-key commercial warmth.

## Park Chan-wook

- Signature: lacquer black and forest green opposed by wine red, ivory/cream skin fields, and cold metal accents.
- Default recipe: 30–40% black/deep green, 15–25% wine red, 20–25% cream/ivory, 10–20% cold teal/steel.
- Exposure: sculpted shadow, polished color separation, protected highlights, precise high-chroma accents.
- Carriers: walls, velvet, lacquered wood, tile, wardrobe, flowers, blood only when narratively required, metallic objects.
- Avoid: random luxury-ad palette, red-and-green without spatial logic, gore used as color decoration.

## Stanley Kubrick

- Signature: choose one rigorous color system per frame, then organize it architecturally.
- Sterile system: 55–70% white/gray, 15–25% black, 5–15% saturated red or blue structural accent.
- Warm historical system: 45–60% candle gold/ochre, 25–35% brown/black, 5–10% muted red or blue.
- Exposure: exact whites and blacks for sterile spaces; dense practical-source warmth for historical spaces.
- Carriers: walls, floors, uniforms, furniture, practical fixtures, corridor geometry.
- Avoid: mixing several systems, decorative rainbow color, accent without architectural purpose.

## David Fincher

- Signature: sickly yellow-green or olive shadows, steel/cyan support, sodium amber practicals, dense neutral blacks.
- Default recipe: 30–40% olive/yellow-green, 20–30% steel/cyan, 20–30% charcoal/black, 8–15% sodium amber.
- Exposure: low-key, protected highlights, readable dark detail, controlled saturation, precise skin isolation.
- Carriers: walls, office fluorescents, windows, street vapor, monitors, clothing, reflective metal.
- Avoid: crushed featureless blacks, vivid emerald fantasy, generic teal-orange action grade.

## Wes Anderson

- Signature: large tactile pastel color blocks, complementary but dusty hue pairs, warm cream structure, and one darker grounding color.
- Default recipe: 30–40% dusty mint or powder blue, 20–30% warm cream, 12–20% mustard, 8–15% brick red or faded coral, 10–20% dark brown/charcoal grounding.
- Alternate recipe: dusty rose + pale teal + burgundy + cream. Choose one recipe only.
- Exposure: even designed light, clean midtones, restrained contrast, clearly separated color blocks.
- Carriers: walls, benches, doors, wardrobe, luggage, upholstery, floor tile, practical fixtures.
- Avoid: random candy colors, low-saturation beige wash, copying a known hotel palette, cute color without emotional function.

## Christopher Nolan

- Signature: photochemical steel/charcoal realism, cool sky or architecture, warm skin and tungsten, one restrained hazard accent.
- Default recipe: 40–50% steel blue/charcoal, 20–30% neutral stone/gray, 15–20% warm skin/tungsten, 3–8% amber/orange safety accent.
- Exposure: dense blacks, strong natural-light separation, large-format clarity, restrained saturation.
- Carriers: sky, concrete, suits, vehicles, practical lamps, fire or warning objects only when motivated.
- Avoid: teal-orange blockbuster excess, blue monochrome, glossy digital HDR.

## Denis Villeneuve

- Signature: one dominant environmental hue covering most of the frame, with a restrained counter-temperature and very small human accent.
- Fog system: 55–70% fog cyan/blue-gray, 20–30% charcoal, 5–10% warm skin or amber.
- Mineral system: 55–70% sand/ochre/mineral beige, 20–30% black/brown, 3–8% cool blue or red human accent.
- Exposure: monumental tonal fields, silhouette clarity, atmospheric roll-off, controlled saturation.
- Carriers: sky, haze, architecture, terrain, wardrobe silhouette, sparse practical light.
- Avoid: busy multi-color palettes, generic orange dust, accent colors competing with environmental scale.

## Sofia Coppola

- Signature: powder pink, pale cyan, lavender-gray, warm cream, soft skin and luminous practical gold.
- Default recipe: 25–35% pale cyan, 20–30% powder pink, 20–30% cream/off-white, 10–20% lavender-gray, 5–10% warm gold.
- Exposure: gentle overexposure, pastel night ambience, soft highlight bloom, low-to-medium contrast.
- Carriers: windows, bedding, hotel/apartment walls, dusk sky, wardrobe, lamps, reflective surfaces.
- Avoid: fashion-campaign saturation, sugary pink dominance, harsh black contrast, glamour skin retouching.

## Akira Kurosawa

- Signature: disciplined weather-driven neutrals with one group, costume, or heraldic accent separating moral forces.
- Default recipe: 40–55% storm gray/earth, 20–30% black/white cloth, 10–20% muted green or straw, 3–8% red/blue/gold accent.
- Exposure: hard tonal separation, readable weather layers, dense blacks, bright rain/snow/dust edges.
- Carriers: sky, mud, cloth, smoke, timber, banners, armor-like material only when original and narratively justified.
- Avoid: ornamental saturation, undirected weather, direct period-film iconography.

## Andrei Tarkovsky

- Signature: mineral gray, moss green, wet brown, oxidized metal, and muted gold embedded in moisture and decay.
- Default recipe: 40–50% mineral gray, 20–30% moss/olive, 15–25% wet umber, 3–8% rust or dim gold.
- Exposure: low contrast, moist highlights, soft window falloff, blacks that retain material detail.
- Carriers: water, plaster, leaves, metal, wood, smoke, fire, aged fabric.
- Avoid: sepia nostalgia filter, random dream color, glossy wet surfaces.

## Ingmar Bergman

- Signature: bone, charcoal, flesh, black, and a restrained cool or blood-warm accent organized around faces.
- Default recipe: 35–50% charcoal/black, 20–30% bone/gray, 20–30% natural flesh, 3–8% muted blue or deep red.
- Exposure: sculpted facial half-tones, exact eye highlights, deep background separation, tactile grain.
- Carriers: skin, linen, dark rooms, shore sky, plain walls, sparse wardrobe.
- Avoid: beauty lighting, cyan-orange portrait grade, decorative colored backgrounds.

## Federico Fellini

- Signature: smoky monochrome structure interrupted by circus red, powder blue, cream, silver, and theatrical amber.
- Default recipe: 30–40% smoke gray/black, 20–30% cream, 10–20% powder blue, 8–15% circus red, 5–10% amber/silver.
- Exposure: luminous haze, hard practical pools, stage-like highlights, dense but playful blacks.
- Carriers: painted sets, costume fabric, makeup kept human, bulbs, smoke, reflective trim.
- Avoid: random rainbow carnival, synthetic neon, color without social or emotional hierarchy.

## Alfred Hitchcock

- Signature: controlled cream and charcoal base, cool green/blue tension, and one precise warning red or yellow clue.
- Default recipe: 35–45% cream/neutral, 20–30% charcoal, 15–25% cool green or slate blue, 3–8% warning red/yellow.
- Exposure: clean motivated contrast, readable shadows, isolated clue highlight, protected faces.
- Carriers: walls, wardrobe, stair rails, windows, vehicles, one causal object.
- Avoid: noir monochrome by default, indiscriminate red, decorative suspense lighting.

## Sergio Leone

- Signature: sun-baked ochre and tobacco brown against faded blue, iron black, and a small dusty red accent.
- Default recipe: 40–55% ochre/sand, 20–30% tobacco brown, 10–20% faded sky blue, 5–10% iron black, 3–6% dusty red.
- Exposure: hard sun, dense shade, hot highlights, dust-visible backlight, tactile skin without glamour.
- Carriers: earth, sky, worn cloth, timber, oxidized metal, sweat, dust.
- Avoid: orange wash, clean costume color, teal-orange western advertising.

## Agnès Varda

- Signature: found color anchored in documentary neutrals—sea blue, sun yellow, human red, concrete, skin, and patterned fabric.
- Default recipe: 35–45% concrete/skin/earth neutral, 20–30% blue, 10–20% yellow, 5–10% red, 10–20% found green or fabric pattern.
- Exposure: honest daylight, open shadows, bright but plausible primaries, tactile grain.
- Carriers: streets, markets, clothing, painted doors, fruit, sea, ordinary objects.
- Avoid: curated influencer palette, whimsical pastel wash, commercial saturation.

## Chantal Akerman

- Signature: restrained beige, institutional green/blue, brown, off-white, and one ordinary red object within flat duration.
- Default recipe: 35–50% beige/off-white, 20–30% muted green or blue, 15–25% brown/gray, 3–6% plain red.
- Exposure: flat available daylight or fluorescent practicals, stable midtones, minimal color cast.
- Carriers: walls, kitchen surfaces, streets, tables, coats, curtains, repeated domestic objects.
- Avoid: dramatic contrast, decorative color blocking, sentimental warm grade.

## Bong Joon-ho

- Signature: class-coded contrast between concrete/cyan institutional space, damp green, warm domestic amber, and one saturated clue.
- Default recipe: 30–40% concrete gray, 20–30% damp green/cyan, 15–25% warm amber/brown, 15–25% charcoal, 3–7% red/yellow clue.
- Exposure: spatially readable shadows, practical highlights, rain or fluorescent separation, neutral skin protected.
- Carriers: architecture levels, windows, tile, household fabric, street water, uniforms kept original.
- Avoid: generic thriller teal-orange, muddy class symbolism, copied basement color systems.

## Pedro Almodóvar

- Signature: saturated vermilion, cobalt, mustard, emerald, black, and cream organized as precise emotional color blocks.
- Default recipe: 25–35% vermilion or cobalt dominant, 20–30% cream, 10–20% secondary primary color, 10–20% black, 5–10% contrasting accent.
- Exposure: clean midtones, soft frontal skin, polished but motivated interiors, crisp color separation.
- Carriers: walls, tile, upholstery, wardrobe, flowers, lacquer, printed fabric, hero objects.
- Avoid: using every primary equally, fashion-ad gloss, color detached from character emotion.

## Terrence Malick

- Signature: living green, wheat gold, sky cyan, earth brown, natural skin, and luminous sun-white highlights.
- Default recipe: 30–45% living green or wheat gold, 20–35% sky cyan, 15–25% earth/wood, 10–20% natural skin/cloth, 3–8% sun-white.
- Exposure: backlit natural dynamic range, luminous roll-off, open landscape shadows, motivated flare only.
- Carriers: vegetation, sky, water, skin, linen, soil, weather.
- Avoid: universal golden-hour filter, radioactive green, constant flare, travel-commercial polish.

## Zhang Yimou

- Signature: one dominant ceremonial color field opposed by black, earth, gold, indigo, or snow-white structure.
- Vermilion system: 55–70% vermilion, 20–30% black/charcoal, 5–10% gold or skin.
- Indigo system: 50–65% indigo/blue-gray, 20–30% earth/black, 5–10% warm skin or red accent.
- Exposure: bold graphic separation, deep ceremonial clarity, hard sun, fog, snow, fire, or fabric bounce.
- Avoid: mixing multiple systems, decorative rainbow spectacle, copying palace or martial color fields.

## Blending and Failure Rules

- For one director, use one recipe only. Do not average all of a profile's variants.
- For two directors, let the primary director control dominant/support/neutral and the secondary director control only the accent or exposure behavior.
- Keep the accent near a narrative anchor, not scattered across decorative props.
- At thumbnail size, dominant/support contrast must remain visible.
- Regenerate once if the frame looks generically beige, generically teal-orange, washed out, rainbow-colored, or dependent on a global tint rather than physical color carriers.
- Strong recognizability must not override skin plausibility, motivated light, narrative tone, or originality boundaries.
